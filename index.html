How to use AU Studio
1

Open the site

Go to https://anpark101.github.io/for-au/ on your phone (or computer). Nothing to install.

2

Pick a platform

Tap Start writing, choose iMessage, Instagram, X, TikTok or any other mock platform.

3

Fill in the blanks

Change names, handles, message text, times, likes, stats… The phone preview updates live as you type.

4

It saves automatically

Your draft is saved to your phone right away — you'll see Saved to device. Close the tab and come back later, it's still there under My projects.

5

Save or share

Tap Export image to save a picture of your post. For a real backup (your "cloud save"), tap Export project to download a .json file — keep it in Google Drive, iCloud Drive, or email it to yourself. Use Import project to bring it back.

Questions

Where is my work saved?

In your browser on this device only (local storage). It is not on a server, so if you clear your browser data it will be gone — always keep a .json backup.

Can I use this on a computer?

Yes. Open the same link; it works on any browser. The preview looks like an iPhone either way.

Is this real social media?

No. These are mock, fictional screens for self-indulgent AU creativity. Nothing is posted anywhere.
